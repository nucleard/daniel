CASCADES
surfaces render at 2× the GI resolution; light is computed low-res and interpolated.
LIVING BLOBS
blobs live 45–95s, shrink away, and are reborn elsewhere in a new color. frost dissolves their outlines into their own glow — a side experiment, very optional, not core to the technique.
TYPE
text always occludes and blushes in the sim; ink overlays the chosen color solidly on top (1 = pure graphic ink, 0 = fully scene-lit).
BRUSH
draw on the canvas. emitters glow, occluders are white surfaces that catch light.
