a stored pair is the full current board — drone sliders + RESOLUTION + RING CHARACTER — plus the computed voicing recipe (per-voice start tone, cadence target, just frequency, lead/tune cents, saw spread, pan, filter base, shimmer rates). IMPORT JSON accepts a book file too and appends its temples.
The composition renders offline into clean, seam-wrapped loop stems; everything you hear plays through a live damage chain the sliders write to directly. EXPORT WAV re-runs the identical chain offline with all modulators quantized to whole cycles per loop — same seed + same params → identical file. RESOLUTION: the drone is treated as a mistuned version of a just-intonation chord it never quite plays. Every oscillator in the live resolution bed sits AT the just frequency, with all mistuning held in its detune parameter (cents) — so RESOLVE is nothing but a ramp of every detune to zero while the damage chain goes transparent. The beats do not fade out; they slow down and stop. EXPORT WAV captures the drone loop only; the resolve is a live event.
