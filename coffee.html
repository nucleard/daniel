Twenty years in coffee

I started on the bar in 2003 and I run a roastery today. In between I taught roasting and cupping, worked with farmers in a dozen countries, started an importing company that failed, and built two roasteries from nothing.

2003 – 2006

On the bar

Barista in Seattle. Three years of espresso, dialling in, and learning what good coffee actually tastes like before anyone paid me to have an opinion about it.

2006 – 2009

New York

Went independent. Started with barista training, moved quickly into roasting consultancy for cafés and small roasters around the city, such as Cafe Grumpy and Intelligentsia Coffee. Became the assistant of Willem Boot of Boot Coffee Consulting, and through him did my first origin work in El Salvador and Ethiopia.

2010 – 2015

Boot Coffee and the road

Back in Seattle, full-time consultant. Taught roasting and cupping courses at the Boot Coffee lab in Mill Valley, and spent the rest of the year at origin: leading buyer tours, training farmers to cup their own coffee, running variety and soil studies.

I did dozens of projects, but here are a few I'm particularly proud of.

Ethiopia, Honduras

The Cupping Caravan. Buyers from across the consuming world in 4x4s through rural producing communities. We brought power inverters, sample roasters and full cupping kits, roasted each community's coffee in front of them and cupped it there, sometimes with five hundred people standing around us like an amphitheatre. Repeated in Honduras.

Puerto Rico

A variety and soil study with Willem Boot for the USDA. Samples from dozens of producers, identical processing, soil analysis by the university, then three large blind cuppings in San Francisco. The result told the Puerto Rican government which varieties do best in which soils, so they could advise farmers what to plant.

Mindanao

A wild Mokka in Bukidnon. With Filipino colleagues, high on a hillside in a remote tribal area, we found a distinct variety growing half-wild. I was on the cupping team that helped bring it to market in Japan.

Ethiopia, 2013

Head judge, Taste of Harvest.

2015 – 2016

Importing, Southeast Asia

Co-founded Origin Coffee Network, a green coffee importing company incorporated in Singapore, with partners in Singapore, Manila and Bangkok. I ran the Philippines side from Manila. We imported green coffee from Panama and Brazil and sold throughout Southeast Asia. We ran out of money after two years. I learned more about the trade in those two years than in any other two. A kind of beautiful failure.

2017

A year out

Away from coffee entirely. I lived on an island in Washington State, made blackberry jam, swam in the Sound every morning and wrote pseudonymous novels published on Amazon.

2018

Bali

Set up Coffee & Coconuts' roastery and café in Bali.

2019 – today

Amsterdam

Head Roaster, Coffee & Coconuts. Built the Amsterdam roastery in 2020 and have run it since: sourcing, profiling, QC, production. Went from just 1,000 kg roasted in our first rough year, during Covid, to a pace of well over 40,000 kg in 2026.

In this role I continue to visit origin, build contacts with producers, select the coffees, set the profiles, and serve as a public face presenting our coffees to the exploding Dutch specialty market.
